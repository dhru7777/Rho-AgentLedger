AgentLedger : Agentic payments, financial evidence and scoring

- Agents transact through Circle or Stripe. AgentLedger integrates Rho transaction data with commerce records, agent identity and wallet activity to reconcile transactions and calculate prototype agent scores.

- Rho provides financial transaction data. AgentLedger performs the reconciliation, scoring and visualization. The integration supports live Rho data and locally generated demo records.

Purchase policy

The policy checks seller identity, the user's spend cap, recent seller failures, price, delivery timing and objectivity. The calculated credit score is not an input to this function.

Drag or scroll to pan 
flowchart LR
  Start["evaluatePolicy"] --> Identity{"Seller identity verified?"}
  Identity -->|no| Reject["REJECT"]
  Identity -->|yes| Spend{"Price exceeds maxSpendUsd?"}
  Spend -->|yes| Reject
  Spend -->|no| Failures{"recentFailures at least 3?"}
  Failures -->|yes| Reject
  Failures -->|no| Protect{"Price at least protectMinUsd
OR subjective result
OR lagged delivery?"}
  Protect -->|yes| Protected["APPROVE
rail = PROTECTED"]
  Protect -->|no| Nano{"Price at most nanoMaxUsd
AND instant delivery
AND objective result?"}
  Nano -->|yes| Direct["APPROVE
rail = DIRECT"]
  Nano -->|no| Protected

Configuration defaults : protectMinUsd = $100 and nanoMaxUsd = $1.

After approval, fiat purchases use the Stripe branch & crypto purchases use the returned DIRECT or PROTECTED method.
